About Our Project

About Bentexto (বাংলা কনটেক্সটো)

Bentexto is the premier Bengali word guessing and contextual deduction game (বাংলা কনটেক্সটো ও শব্দ মেলানোর খেলা). Inspired by viral linguistic games like Contexto and Wordle, Bentexto was built to celebrate and expand the reach of the rich Bengali language (বাংলা ভাষা) across the world.

Our Mission & Educational Vision

With over 300 million speakers, Bengali is one of the most widely spoken and culturally vibrant languages on Earth. However, modern digital word puzzle games in Bengali have historically been scarce. Bentexto bridges this gap by offering a high-quality, free, and accessible daily brain puzzle where players discover new vocabulary, test linguistic associations, and challenge friends.

How the Technology Works

Instead of matching letters or spelling patterns, Bentexto calculates semantic proximity. Using natural language processing algorithms and semantic word embeddings trained on vast Bengali text corpora, the engine determines how conceptually similar your guess is to the secret daily target word.

  • Rank 1: The secret winning word of the day!
  • Ranks 2 - 300 (Green): Concepts that are intimately linked (e.g., if the target is বৃষ্টি (rain), then মেঘ (cloud) or জল (water) will rank very close).
  • Ranks 301 - 1,500 (Yellow): Moderately related vocabulary.
  • Ranks 1,501+ (Red): Distant concepts.

Built-In Phonetic Bangla Transliteration

To ensure Bentexto is accessible to everyone regardless of their keyboard setup, we built an automatic phonetic typing engine. You can simply type in English (for example, typing bristi produces বৃষ্টি, and krishok produces কৃষক) or use our virtual on-screen Bengali keyboard with complete ligature (যুক্তাক্ষর) support.

Transparency & Google AdSense Advertising

Bentexto is 100% free to access. We display non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdSense to offset cloud server hosting, database maintenance, and continuous vocabulary expansion. We strictly adhere to Google Publisher Policies and user privacy safeguards.
