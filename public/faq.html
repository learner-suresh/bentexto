Frequently Asked Questions (Bentexto FAQ)

1. What is Bentexto and how is it related to Contexto?
Bentexto is the authentic Bengali adaptation of the worldwide viral game Contexto. While the original Contexto operates in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, Bentexto was specially developed for the Bengali language. It uses natural language processing models to rank every guessed Bengali word by its semantic proximity to a secret daily word.
2. What does Rank #1 mean?
Rank #1 is the secret winning word of the day! The closer your guess is to 1, the closer in meaning and contextual association your word is to the hidden target. Lower numbers represent higher similarity.
3. Is there a limit to how many guesses I can make?
No! Bentexto offers unlimited guesses every single day. There is no penalty for trying multiple words. You can experiment with different word associations until you solve the puzzle.
4. When does the daily secret word refresh?
A brand-new Daily Secret Word puzzle unlocks every night at midnight (00:00 local time). You can see the countdown timer at the bottom of the screen. In addition, you can practice indefinitely using our Practice Mode or explore past games through the Archive menu.
5. Do I need to create an account or sign up to play?
No registration or sign-up is required. Bentexto is completely free to play. Your daily streak, win rates, and guess histories are saved securely in your browser's local storage.
6. How does the phonetic typing work?
Our built-in transliteration tool lets you type Bengali words using standard English QWERTY keyboard letters. For instance, typing bristi generates বৃষ্টি, jol generates জল, and shurjo generates সূর্য. You can also switch to direct Bengali input or use the on-screen virtual keyboard.
7. How does Bentexto support itself?
Bentexto is maintained as an open educational project. To keep cloud servers, NLP linguistic computation, and dictionary curation 100% free for users worldwide, we partner with transparent advertising networks like Google AdSense. We do not sell user data.
8. How can I suggest new Bengali words or report an issue?
We welcome community contributions from linguists, educators, and players! You can contact us anytime via email at contact.bentexto@gmail.com.
