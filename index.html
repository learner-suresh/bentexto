Bentexto — Bengali Contexto & Daily Bengali Word Guessing Game (বাংলা কনটেক্সটো)

Bentexto (বেনটেক্সটো) is the official Bengali semantic word guessing game inspired by Contexto and Wordle. Every day, players try to uncover a secret target Bengali word (আজকের গোপন বাংলা শব্দ) using semantic artificial intelligence proximity ranking.

How to Play Bengali Contexto (বাংলা কনটেক্সটো খেলার নিয়ম)

  • Type any Bengali word or write phonetically in English (e.g., typing "bristi" suggests "বৃষ্টি", "jol" suggests "জল").
  • The secret word is ranked #1. Words conceptually or contextually closer have lower rank numbers and glow green.
  • Enjoy unlimited guesses every single day with daily streak counters and infinite randomized practice games.

Features of Bentexto

Includes phonetic English-to-Bangla auto-transliteration, interactive Bengali virtual keyboard, detailed hints, daily archives, dark and light theme support, and zero sign-up requirements.
