What is Bengali Contexto? (বাংলা কনটেক্সটো কি?)

Bentexto is the authentic Bengali adaptation of the global Contexto sensation. Instead of matching letters like Wordle, Bentexto uses semantic artificial intelligence to evaluate the conceptual closeness of your guessed word to today's secret Bengali word.

How to Play the Bengali Word Guessing Game

  • Guess Any Bengali Word: Type a word in Bengali or use phonetic English (e.g. type "bristi" to get বৃষ্টি, or "jol" to get জল).
  • AI Semantic Ranking: The secret target word is ranked #1. Words closer in context receive lower ranks (green), while unrelated words get higher ranks.
  • Unlimited Guesses: Guess as many words as you need to uncover the secret word.
  • Daily Game & Unlimited Practice: Play the official Daily Puzzle or practice with infinite randomized words anytime.
